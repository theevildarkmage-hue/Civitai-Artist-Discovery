# Discovery 3.0 privacy notice

Prepared for the stable Production release. Production Community and Registry are
disabled during private candidate acceptance; public participation begins only
after the separately approved launch. Published beta environments are separate.

## Local application data

Discovery keeps gallery/history SQLite data, preferences, imported content controls,
reactions/follows metadata, and caches in its local profile. Civitai OAuth access
and refresh credentials remain local. Windows protects credential storage with
DPAPI; experimental Linux source use requires Secret Service. Ordinary settings,
artwork caches and SQLite history are not claimed encrypted. Keep the profile
private and do not attach it to public issues or release assets.

## Community and identity

When public Community is enabled, normal Civitai sign-in supplies a signed identity
assertion transiently over HTTPS. The service verifies its issuer, audience, nonce,
signature and expiry and obtains the public username. It does not receive the
user's Civitai API access or refresh token. Successful public enrollment creates or
updates a Community member record containing the verified numeric Civitai ID and
public username; repeated login updates that row without a second registration.
Membership is not an exemption from authentication, blocks, quotas or moderation.

Discovery-only access sessions last up to twelve hours. Rotating authorization is
bound to a local P-256 device key, with a 30-day idle and 90-day absolute lifetime.
The service stores identity/time/revocation data, public device keys and hashes of
Discovery credentials, assertions/nonces and replay proofs. Raw session/renewal
credentials and private keys remain protected locally. Expired operational rows
are cleaned in bounded batches on later activity; there is no guaranteed timed
purge or promise that expiry immediately deletes every related record.

Recommendations store artwork and artist IDs, submitter identity, acceptance time,
UTC quota week and status. The feed shows public artwork attribution and the
recommender's public username. The server also retains idempotency, rate-limit,
moderation/report/block and security-audit data needed for integrity and abuse
handling. Ordinary clients cannot enumerate protected tables or administrative
records. The infrastructure owner and hosting provider have their respective
administrative access.

Seven-day feed eligibility is not deletion. Withdrawal/moderation removes active
visibility but preserves quota, replay/idempotency and audit history. Account or
attribution deletion requests require dependency-aware review rather than a
blanket database reset. No automatic data transfer from the Development beta is
part of stable launch.

## Separate application registry

Registry collection is independently controlled and initially disabled in
Production. If the owner separately activates it, authenticated use registers six
fields: verified account ID, public username, first seen, last seen, app version
and platform. Activity updates occur at most once per twelve hours; version and
platform are client-reported. The registry does not authorize Community access.
Stopping use stops client heartbeats; later authenticated use can recreate a
deleted registry entry. Registry deletion alone does not erase Community/audit
history. No new consent interface or separate account is introduced by this notice.

The local application contacts Civitai for authentication, public artwork and user
requested social operations. Supabase hosts Community/identity and any enabled
registry. GitHub supplies release metadata/downloads. These providers process
ordinary connection metadata under their own policies; the application does not
promise that infrastructure logs contain no IP addresses or that disabling a
feature eliminates all provider traffic.

## Retention, backups and requests

Local data remains under the user's control until removed. Removing the local
installation does not erase hosted records. The current operator is the repository
owner. Routine operational log/throttle retention has a 90-day target, not an
implemented universal deletion schedule. Other record retention is reviewed for
operational/security needs; no automatic purge or response-time SLA is promised.
Provider log retention can be shorter than this operational target.

Application recovery uses encrypted Windows-account-bound backups. A backup can
retain historical records until the operator retires it; immediate erasure from
all archives is not promised. Current recovery excludes complete provider Auth,
Storage, secret and platform restoration. Backups, private logs and request
evidence are kept out of public release assets.

For sensitive privacy or data-deletion requests, contact
[theevildarkmage@gmail.com](mailto:theevildarkmage@gmail.com), confirmed by the owner
for public use. Provide the minimum account reference and requested scope; do not
email OAuth/session tokens, private device keys, passwords, databases or backups.
The owner verifies identity through a fresh authorized sign-in or equivalent
narrow evidence before acting. Review may retain required quota/audit/security
history or explain scope limitations. Civitai account deletion is handled by Civitai.
