# Beta privacy and data-access inventory

The intended beta client automatically schedules registration after authenticated
Civitai use. There is no opt-in, consent preference file, checkbox, popup or banner.
Owner-only hosted acceptance passed on 2026-10-23. Hosted collection remains off;
acceptance does not authorize general collection or distribution.
The concise notice is provided before installation in the repository README,
Windows/Linux beta guides and release notes. No public distribution is authorized.

## Application disclosure

After successful Civitai login, if hosted collection is separately activated,
the hosted handler independently verifies your account through Civitai UserInfo.
It registers the stable public account ID, current public username, first verified
use, most recent authenticated activity, reported app version and reported
platform (Windows/Linux/macOS/Other). Version/platform are informational and never
grant access. Anonymous installations are not registered. Registry registration
does not create a Community membership or invitation and does not use weekly
recommendation quota.

Activity is updated at most once every 12 hours. A local bounded scheduler keeps
no durable retry queue and backs off for two hours on failure. The database also
enforces the 12-hour interval across devices/instances. A backend outage does not
block Daily Gallery or change personal data. Username correction follows fresh
UserInfo at the next permitted heartbeat, within 12 hours of active verified use;
first_seen remains unchanged. No caller-provided identity or username is accepted.

The registry stores no OAuth/refresh tokens, passwords, IP addresses, hardware or
device identifiers, or browsing history. OAuth is sent transiently over HTTPS for
identity verification, never stored in registry rows or application logs. Local
OAuth is protected by Windows DPAPI or Linux Secret Service. Local gallery history,
reactions, filters and preferences remain in the personal data directory.

Only the local owner-operated administrator can request registry usage
aggregates through private owner administration. Ordinary users, client keys, Community
invitations and runtime service-role credentials do not grant registry enumeration
or administrative authority. Project database administrators retain underlying
administrative access and must follow the incident/privacy runbook.

## Retention policy: approved decisions and remaining proposals

The owner approved GitHub Issues for general privacy/support contact and a 90-day
target for routine operational logs and throttle records. The owner is the initial
operator; no more than five testers may be invited after separate approval. Other
periods below remain proposals. This is not an approved purge job or a promise of
automatic deletion. No hosted records are deleted by this milestone. Do not change grants, reset quotas or run generic SQL
deletions to implement this table.

| Invitations | Verified Civitai ID, expiry/revocation and owner action; private backend/operator | Invitations expire within 30 days unless renewed; proposed deletion review 90 days after expiry/revocation, retaining required audit references |
| Blocks | Verified Civitai ID and enforcement/reason; private backend/operator | While blocked, then proposed 90 days after unblock or beta closure; do not expire an active abuse block silently |
| Reports | Recommendation/reporter IDs, fixed reason, time and decision; private operator queue, reporter never public | Open until resolved; proposed triage within 24 hours and resolved-report retention 90 days; owner availability/incident escalation required |

| Routine operational logs | Diagnostics and incident investigation; restricted owner/provider access | Approved 90-day target; no application-wide 90-day purge or archive is implemented. Provider retention differs and is not changed here |

Incident holds require explicit owner scope and review date |

Registry inactivity is measured from last_seen, with proposed monthly owner review
of rows older than 180 days through the existing restricted retention action.
Other purge capabilities and relational dependency rules have not been implemented
or accepted on hosted data. Approving periods does not authorize a migration or
deletion operation. Irreversibility and audit/replay dependencies need a separate
reviewed cleanup plan; a hold must not become indefinite retention by accident.

Local profile data (history, reactions, follows, preferences and encrypted OAuth)
is controlled by the user and retained until they remove it. Removing the portable
installation does not remove hosted registry/Community records. This task neither
reads existing OAuth secrets nor changes existing installations.

For general support and nonsensitive privacy questions, use
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/theevildarkmage-hue/Civitai-Artist-Discovery/issues).
Do not post tokens, OAuth/session files, account details, reporter identities or
sensitive deletion/security requests in public issues. A minimal issue may ask
the maintainer how to establish private contact without disclosing the details.

**Distribution blocker:** an approved private privacy/security mechanism is not
yet designated. Proposed security channel: GitHub private vulnerability reporting,
only after the owner approves and verifies it is enabled. It is not a general
privacy-request inbox. The owner must separately designate a private privacy
channel; no address is invented or inferred from unrelated account information.
See [GitHub private reports](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/how-tos/report-and-fix-vulnerabilities/report-privately).

The owner verifies the requester's Civitai identity through an authorized fresh
login or equivalent narrow evidence, then executes owner-authorized `registry_delete`
for that stable ID with a nonsecret reason. Verify removal through restricted
administration. There is no app-side consent preference to withdraw. Later
authenticated use may recreate the registry entry; stopping use prevents new
client heartbeats. Registry deletion does not silently remove Community history
or audit evidence; review their dependencies and applicable obligations separately.

Proposed procedure: acknowledge within two business days, verify the stable account
privately using a fresh authorized login, document the requested scope, execute
only approved restricted actions and report completion/retained exceptions within
30 days or a shorter applicable deadline. These are proposed service targets,
not a determination of legal requirements. Username corrections use fresh UserInfo;
version/platform are client-reported, not verified personal identity attributes.
Community withdrawal changes status and removes the active gallery entry; it does
not erase weekly quota, replay protection or prior moderation/audit evidence.
Requests to remove public attribution or linked Community history require an
explicit dependency-aware decision; the existing registry_delete action alone
does not implement that broader deletion. Civitai account deletion is performed
at Civitai, not by deleting this application's registry row.

An immutable encrypted backup may retain a deleted row until its approved expiry.
Do not promise immediate erasure from every archive or modify historical archives
silently. The owner must approve a restricted deletion-obligation record containing
only necessary account/target IDs and request/effective dates; any recovery must
reapply outstanding deletions before reactivation. That record/custody procedure is
not provisioned here. Separate holds and provider logs may have different limits.
Throttle TTL is opportunistic cleanup on a later rate check, not a background
two-minute erasure guarantee. With no new requests an old bucket can remain stored.
The approved 90-day target is an operational policy, not current 90-day storage
or deletion enforcement. No new archive, scheduled cleanup, provider-setting
change or retention migration is introduced. Before claiming enforcement, approve
a separate plan or explicitly accept the shorter current throttle lifecycle.
Public GitHub Issues have their own public record lifecycle and are not covered
by a promise to delete local routine logs after 90 days.

## Provider logging boundary

Supabase API gateways, Auth, PostgreSQL and Edge infrastructure may independently
record request/network metadata, including IP addresses. The registry exclusion
does not imply infrastructure anonymity. The current Free plan's documented API
and database log retention is one day; the exact project-specific Auth/Edge
retention and any provider internal/security retention were not independently
verified. Project Owner/Admin/Developer and provider-authorized personnel can have
log access under their respective permissions. Do not export raw logs with tokens
or personal network metadata into reports. No provider logs were retrieved here.
See [pricing](https://supabase.com/pricing) and
[log sources/access](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/observability/logs).

## Legal and operational applicability

No specific requirement for consent UI has been established for this beta; tester
jurisdictions and the operator's legal circumstances have not been supplied. This
notice is not a legal-compliance certification or a claim that installing software
constitutes valid consent. Where GDPR applies, processing needs an appropriate
lawful basis, and required transparency includes controller/contact information,
purpose/basis, retention and rights. Consent is only one possible lawful basis.
Those facts and any jurisdiction-specific obligations must be settled before
applicable distribution/collection; a short generic notice alone cannot supply
missing controller or legal-basis facts. Address any specifically applicable gap
in the pre-install documentation and operating procedure, not by inventing new UI.
See [EDPB lawful bases](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/topics/key-gdpr-concepts/legal-basis_en)
and [Commission transparency information](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-individuals_en).

GitHub Issues is the approved general contact. The owner still needs to define the private contact channel in each beta
invitation, final retention/correction/deletion obligations, backup custody/RPO/RTO
and operator coverage. These are operational/distribution decisions. Registry
collection on staging stays disabled while deployment and acceptance are pending;
no frontend consent mechanism is required or implemented.
