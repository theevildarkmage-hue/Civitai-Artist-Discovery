WELCOME

Sign in to Civitai

This app is built around your taste — the artwork you react to decides which creators it puts in front of you. Signing in reads that history so the gallery can be ordered for you rather than by popularity, and lets you follow and react without leaving the app.

  • Civitai asks what to allow. Follows and reactions happen only when you click them.
  • Your history is analysed on this computer and never uploaded.
  • You can sign out at any time. Your saved analysis stays on this computer.

This app has no Civitai application of its own, on purpose — that would route everyone's access through one person's account. Connecting takes a one-time setup of your own, and everything then runs under your registration.

  1. On Civitai, open Account settings → OAuth Applications and choose Register App.
  2. Give it any name, and set the redirect URI to http://localhost:8765/oauth/callback exactly.
  3. Register it as public. There is no client secret to copy.
  4. Paste the client ID below.
